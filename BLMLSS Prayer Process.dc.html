Optional Resources

If you're interested in learning more, here are some additional resources we've found helpful.

Deliverance series by Jon Thompson · 3 teachings
Deliverance – Week 1 – The Unholy Trinity ↗ This message explains the “unholy trinity” of the world, the flesh, and the devil, and how each one works to influence, tempt, and oppose believers. Deliverance – Week 2 – Upstairs & Downstairs Battles ↗ A teaching that distinguishes between “upstairs” battles (the flesh and internal struggle) and “downstairs” battles (direct demonic conflict), showing how each requires a different biblical response. Deliverance – Week 3 – Allegiance, Truth, and Power ↗ This sermon focuses on how deliverance requires right allegiance to Jesus, embracing biblical truth, and relying on God’s power to overcome spiritual bondage.
Books on deliverance
They Shall Expel Demons by Derek Prince ↗ A practical handbook on what demons are, how they gain entry, and how to receive and minister deliverance and remain free. Deliverance: A Journey Toward the Unexpected by Jon Thompson ↗ A theological and practical study that walks through Scripture, church history and the major deliverance models to show how people are set free to live as Jesus promised. Convergence by Jon Thompson ↗ Shows how Jesus modeled ministry through spiritual disciplines, spiritual gifts and the Spirit’s promptings, and how believers and local churches can do the same.
